    What this is

    The smart helmet gives a heat-strain risk warning. It is not a diagnosis. It works from an estimated core temperature, calculated from heart rate, motion and the surroundings, so it can be wrong.

    How to read the state

    • Green: no heat-strain risk warning.
    • Yellow: raised risk.
    • Red: risk warning; the helmet also sounds its buzzer.
    • Fault: a needed sensor is down, or the helmet has no risk output. Check the helmet.
    • Not calibrated: no valid baseline; only the simpler fallback rule runs.

    The state shown is the one the worker sees on the helmet LED. Red probability is empty when the fallback rule decided. A grey "Test point" is a message from a test tool.

    Data source

    The badge at the top says where the data came from: real (a helmet), sim (simulated workers) or test (test tool). Simulated data is never real worker data.

    Limits

    • This is a heat-strain risk warning, not a diagnosis. A worker who feels unwell must be treated as unwell whatever the colour.
    • The core temperature is estimated, not measured.
    • Skin temperature is not shown in this app.
    • Data is at most one point per minute and can arrive late. After a 3 minute silence the app warns that the data is stale.
    • The helmet clock has no battery: if its status says "clock not synchronised", the times can be wrong.

    Data: ThingSpeak channel , read-only. Times are shown in your local time.
